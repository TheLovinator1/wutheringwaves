[How to Update]

Once the maintenance for the version update begins, you can follow the following instructions to update your client.

  • PC: Close the game, restart the launcher, and then click on "Update."

  • Android: Launch the game and follow the instructions in the update pop-up window to complete the update.

  • iOS: Download/update the game in the App Store.

  • Mac: Download/update the game in the App Store.

  • PS5™: Open the home screen > Select Wuthering Waves > Press the OPTIONS button to Check for Update

  • XBOX Series X|S: Open the home screen > Select "My games & apps" > Go to "Manage Updates" to find Wuthering Waves > Update the game.

  • XBOX PC: Open the XBOX app > Select "My Library" > Select Wuthering Waves and open the Manage screen > Go to the Files tab and select Check for Update.

[Pre-download]

✦Pre-download Start Time: 2026-09-28 10:00 (UTC+8)

[Required Pre-download Storage Space]

PC: 46GB (Actual package size: 26GB)

Android/iOS: 20GB (Actual package size: 13GB)

Pre-download may not be possible if your device does not have enough spare storage space. To ensure there is sufficient storage space for resource integration during the update maintenance, a certain amount of space must be reserved. If the reserved space is insufficient, the update maintenance will not be completed.

After the update maintenance is complete, the extra space used for the resources merging process will be automatically freed up.

[How to Pre-download]

PC: When pre-download for the latest version becomes available, "New Version Pre-download" appears below the "Start" button on the launcher interface. Select this option to pre-download the resources.

Android/iOS: When pre-download for the latest version becomes available, press the "Pre-download" button, either on the title screen after a successful login or on the in-game Terminal page, and confirm to pre-download the resources.

You can continue playing the game while the pre-downloading process is ongoing. The process can be paused at any time. Whether it is complete or not, the update maintenance will speed up to a certain degree when initiated.

Please ensure that you have a stable network connection during the pre-downloading process.

[About the Schedule for the "Tiered Client Resource Downloads" Limited Test]

✦Test Participants and Test Schedule

PC (※Official Launcher): The test will become available in stages on PC following the Version 3.7 update.

iOS/Android Mobile: The test will become available on all iOS/Android mobile devices following the Version 3.7 update.

Notes

  • We will soon (during Version 3.6) make a PC launcher update available to some of the Rovers who are granted access to the limited test. However, the "Tiered Client Resource Downloads" feature will only go live after the Version 3.7 update. During Version 3.6, being granted access to the limited test will not affect your current gaming experience.

  • Following the Version 3.7 update, Rovers who have not been granted access to the limited test will have their resource package updated to the High-Definition (HD) version by default. Once you enter the test, you can adjust the resource tier based on your needs.

The feature will also be gradually made available on PC platforms with non-official launchers after the test concludes. Please stay tuned for future announcements.

✦Additional Notes on the Limited Test and Version 3.7 Pre-Download

PC (※Official Launcher):

  • For Rovers who are granted access to the limited test and update the official launcher during Version 3.6, Version 3.7 pre-download will be temporarily unavailable due to cross-version functionality differences. You may select and download a suitable resource tier after the Version 3.7 update.

  • For Rovers who do not gain access to the limited test during Version 3.6, pre-download will remain available, and if they use the pre-download feature, the High-Definition (HD) resource tier will be downloaded by default. After gaining test access in Version 3.7, you can then choose a resource tier based on your needs.

iOS/Android Mobile:

  • Once Version 3.7 pre-download becomes available, only a portion of the resources will be pre-downloaded on iOS and Android mobile devices. The remaining tiered resources will be downloaded separately after the Version 3.7 update, based on the package selected.

The Pre-download feature from Version 3.7 onward will function as usual, and the pre-downloaded resources will be of the same tier as your currently selected resources.
