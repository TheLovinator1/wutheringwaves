Features

Better Autopilot Mode Experience

  • In Autopilot Mode, if the motorbike remains stationary for a certain period of time, the system will automatically reset its position and attempt to resume Autopilot.

  • When the destination is close, the system will now recommend traveling there directly instead of activating Navigation Tracking.

  • Resolved misalignment issues of some icons in Autopilot Mode.

Better Motorbike Performance in Certain Situations

  • When the motorbike's path is blocked by certain small objects in Mengzhou's Land of Xuanfang, the objects will become transparent, and the motorbike will be able to drive on.

Mail

New Feature: Mail Favorites

  • Added the Favorites button at the bottom of a Mail. Use it to favorite the Mail.

  • Favorited Mails will not be deleted when their expiration dates come up or when you select "Delete Read Mails."

  • You can favorite up to 100 mails.

Please note that unfavoriting favorited mails at a date later than their expiration dates will result in their deletion.

Adjusted Mail Function

  • The mailbox can now store a maximum of 200 mails.

  • Increased the number of attachments that can be included in a single mail to 40.

  • Adjusted the logic governing which mails will be auto-deleted first when the limit is reached.

Make sure to claim your attachments promptly to avoid any loss from expiration and deletion.

Graphics & Performance

Graphics Settings

Upgraded NVIDIA DLSS Support to DLSS 4.5.

  • You can enable the 5x or 6x options in DLSS 4.5 frame generation, if your device meets the following requirements:

  1. RTX 50 Series

  2. With DX12 enabled (first enable Hardware-Accelerated GPU Scheduling in your system's graphics settings)

  3. Graphics driver version is 595.97 or newer.

  • If your device meets the requirements, you can select the 5x or 6x options in DLSS 4.5 via Settings > Graphics > Advanced > Frame Generation. You can choose your frame generation rate based on your needs or the configuration of your device.

If the original frame rate is too low, enabling Frame Generation can increase the frame rate. Please note that enabling this option may cause input lag or display issues.

Extended Availability of Vulkan on Mobile

  • Following the Version 3.6 update maintenance, the Vulkan option can also be enabled in the following processors:

  1. Snapdragon 8 Gen 2

  2. Dimensity 9200, 9200+

  3. XRing O1

  • This option is accessible via Settings > Graphics > Vulkan. Please restart the game after enabling the Vulkan option for the change to take effect.

  • Enabling the Vulkan option improves the game's performance and power consumption while it is being run on mobile.

Following the version update, the Vulkan option will now be on by default on mobile devices equipped with Snapdragon 8 Elite, Snapdragon 8 Elite 2, Snapdragon 8 Gen 5, or Dimensity 9500.

  • Extended support for Ray Tracing Megalights to devices equipped with AMD graphics. On devices equipped with AMD graphics that meet the requirements, enabling Ray Tracing improves the lighting effects in certain environments. (※There isn't a separate "Megalights" option. It takes effect when Ray Tracing is enabled.)

  • Extended support for self-developed frame generation to mobile devices equipped with Snapdragon 8 Gen 5. If your processor meets the requirements, you can enable this option via Settings > Graphics > Frame Generation.

Audio

  • Added a tag and timer to some tracks/albums that are available for a limited time. The timer shows how much time a track/album will remain available.

  • Adjusted certain audio files to prevent abnormal triggering.
